Speaking

Mock exam · four tasks
Instructions

Record yourself on your phone, like in the exam. Do the four tasks without stopping. Speak until the time finishes.

1 · Warm Up (1–2 minutes)

  • Talk about your personal details (What's your name? Where are you from? Where do you live? How long have you lived there?)
  • Talk about your occupation (What do you do? Where do you work or study?)
  • Talk about your interests (What do you do in your free time? How often?)
  • Talk about your routines (What do you do on a typical day? What did you do last weekend? What are your plans for the future?)

2 · Reading Aloud (30 seconds to prepare)

Good morning, and welcome to the Annual Sales Conference. Before we begin, I'd like to give you some practical information. Today's programme has three parts. The first session starts at 9:30 in the Main Hall and finishes at 11:15. After a short coffee break, we will divide into four groups for the workshops. Lunch will be served at 1 p.m. in the restaurant on the second floor. Please remember to wear your name badge at all times and to switch off your mobile phone during the presentations. If you need any help, our staff in the green T-shirts will be happy to help you. We hope you have a productive and enjoyable day.

4 · Extended Conversation (about 1½ minutes)

Look at this information comparing two places for a weekend trip. Which one would you prefer? Give reasons for your answer. Then talk about your last holiday. Where did you go? What did you do?

Beach apartment · Torrevieja

2 minutes from the beach. Swimming pool. Restaurants and nightlife nearby. Busy in summer. €140 for two nights.

Mountain cabin · Sierra de Aitana

Quiet, in the middle of the forest. Walking routes and a lake. No restaurants: you cook. 1 hour by car from the nearest town. €110 for two nights.

Model answer

I'd prefer the mountain cabin. It's cheaper than the beach apartment, and it's much quieter, so I think it's better for relaxing after a busy week. I love walking, and the routes and the lake look great. The beach apartment is more convenient, because there are restaurants nearby, but it's very busy in summer and I don't like crowds. My last holiday was in August. I went to Galicia with two friends. We stayed in a small village near the coast, we walked a lot and we ate a lot of fish. It was fantastic, although it rained for two days.

■ comparatives · ■ past simple

Writing

Mock exam · 20 minutes

Read the following e-mail and write a reply as if you are Laura Martín, the customer service manager of SportZone. Make sure you provide all the information that Kevin Hart requests. (Min. 150 words)

To: Laura Martín <customerservice@sportzone.com>
From: Kevin Hart <k.hart@hmail.com>
Subject: Wrong size – order 7712

Dear Ms Martín,

Ten days ago I ordered a pair of running shoes from your website (order 7712). They arrived yesterday, but they are a size 42 and I ordered a size 44.

Could you tell me how I can return them, and if I have to pay for the shipping? I would also like to know when I will receive the correct size. I need them for a half marathon next Sunday.

Finally, as this was your mistake, is it possible to have a discount?

I look forward to your reply.

Kevin Hart

Words: 0 / 150

After 20 minutes: check

Model answer

Dear Mr Hart,

Thank you for your email, and I am very sorry that you received the wrong size. I have checked your order, and you are right: our warehouse sent a size 42 instead of a size 44.

Returning the shoes is very easy. You don't have to pay anything, because we will send a courier to your home to collect them. Could you tell me which day is best for you? The courier can come any day from Monday to Friday, between 9 a.m. and 6 p.m.

We sent your new shoes this morning by express delivery, so you will receive them on Thursday at the latest. That means you will have them before your half marathon.

As an apology for our mistake, we are happy to give you a 20% discount. We have already refunded the money to your card, and you will see it in three to five days.

Good luck in the race! If you have any other questions, please do not hesitate to contact me.

Kind regards,
Laura Martín
Customer Service Manager, SportZone

Exam day

Strategy
Before the exam
  • Sleep well and arrive early.
  • Bring everything your exam centre asks for.
  • Test your headphones and microphone before you start.
Listening
  • Read the options before each audio.
  • Never leave an answer blank: a wrong answer doesn't lose points.
  • If you miss one, choose and move on.
Reading
  • Look at the timer: about 8 minutes per part.
  • Incomplete Sentences: find the clue word first.
  • Text Comprehension: questions 1 and 2 are usually text type and purpose.
Speaking
  • Speak until the time finishes. Silence is worse than mistakes.
  • Use rescue phrases: Let me think… / In other words…
  • Picture: there is / prepositions / present continuous.
Writing
  • 3 minutes to plan, 14 to write, 3 to check.
  • Tick every point in the e-mail.
  • Count your words: 150 or more.
Remember
  • The exam is multilevel: some questions are B2 or C1. Don't panic.
  • Every answer counts for your level.
