Collocations: make · do · have · take
VerbGoes with…
makea decision · a mistake · a suggestion · a complaint · an appointment · a phone call
doa course · overtime · business · your best · a good job · the shopping
havea meeting · a look · lunch · a chat · a problem · a good time
takea break · notes · part in · a photo · the bus · care of

Tip: make is usually for creating or producing something (a decision, a mistake); do is for activities and work (a course, a good job).

although or however?

Although it was late, we finished. (one sentence)
It was late. However, we finished. (new sentence + comma)

because or because of?

Model answer

Dear Ms Carter,

Thank you for your letter and for the information about your customer service courses.

After talking to our managers, we have decided to choose the Online Course. It is cheaper than the Classroom Course, and our employees can carry on working while they study. In addition, they don't have to travel to Manchester.

Twelve employees are going to do the course, so I understand that we can get the 10% discount if we book before 30 November. Could you confirm this, please?

I would also like to ask two questions. When does the next course start, and at what time are the live sessions? And is the final exam online, or do our employees have to take it at your centre?

Finally, I would be happy to set up a video call. Would Tuesday 21 October at 10 a.m. (Spanish time) be possible for you?

I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,
Daniel Ortega
HR Manager, Costa Logistics

The e-mail and the four speaking tasks, like on exam day

Overview

Speaking and Writing on exam day
TaskWhat you doKey to a good score
Warm UpTalk about yourself: personal details, job or studies, free time, routine, last weekend, plans.Use three tenses (present, past, future). Never answer with one word.
Reading AloudRead a short text aloud.Prepare for 30 seconds: mark pauses, numbers and dates.
Picture DescriptionDescribe a photo.There is / there are + prepositions + present continuous. Five steps.

WritingReply to an e-mail. 20 minutes, min. 150 words.Answer every point. Paragraphs, formal greeting and closing.

Writing workshop

Improve a weak e-mail

Read the e-mail and reply as Sara Gil, the office manager. Answer all of Tom's questions. (Min. 150 words)

To: Sara Gil <s.gil@costalogistics.com>
From: Tom Fisher <tom.fisher@hmail.com>
Subject: My first day

Dear Ms Gil,

I'm starting my new job in your office on 3 November. Could you tell me what time I should arrive on my first day and who I should ask for? I'd also like to know if there is a canteen in the building or if I need to bring my lunch. Finally, is it possible to park near the office?

Best regards,
Tom Fisher

About 50 words

What's wrong with it? Tick the problems.
Check the problems

✓ The greeting and closing are too informal.
✓ It doesn't answer the question about parking.
✓ It's too short (about 50 words).
✓ There are no paragraphs.
✓ There are grammar and spelling mistakes.
✗ It uses too many tenses.
✗ It answers questions Tom didn't ask.

Mistakes:
thanks for you email → Thank you for your email
its in the first floor → it's on the first floor
you dont need bring → you don't need to bring
hi / see you → Dear Tom / Kind regards
Ask for me in reception → ask for me at reception

Model answer

Dear Tom,

Thank you for your email. We are all looking forward to meeting you on 3 November.

On your first day, please arrive at 8:45, because we are going to have a short welcome meeting at nine. When you arrive, please go to reception on the ground floor and ask for me. I will show you your desk and introduce you to the team.

There is a canteen on the first floor, so you don't need to bring your lunch. It's open from 12:30 to 3 p.m., and the food is good and quite cheap.

Unfortunately, there isn't a car park in our building, but there is a public car park opposite the office. It costs about €8 a day. However, many of our employees come by train, because the station is only five minutes away.

If you have any other questions, please do not hesitate to contact me.

Kind regards,
Sara Gil
Office Manager

Writing workshop

Exam practice · 20 minutes

Read the following e-mail and write a reply as if you are Marco Silva, the manager of the Green Park Hotel. Make sure you provide all the information that Lisa Grant requests. (Min. 150 words)

To: Marco Silva <events@greenparkhotel.com>
From: Lisa Grant <l.grant@brightway.com>
Subject: Training event in December

Dear Mr Silva,

I'm organising a two-day training event for 20 employees on 12 and 13 December. Could you tell me if you have a meeting room for 20 people and what equipment it has? I'd also like to know the price of the room and lunch for both days.

Finally, we need ten bedrooms for the night of the 12th. Are they available, and is there a discount for groups?

I look forward to hearing from you.

Kind regards,
Lisa Grant
Events Coordinator, Brightway

Answer everything
Plan: 5 paragraphs
  1. Thank her for the email.
  2. The meeting room and its equipment.
  3. Prices for the room and lunch.
  4. Bedrooms and the group discount.
  5. Offer more help and close.

The room has a projector, a screen and…

Please do not hesitate to contact me.

Your answer

Words: 0 / 150
Model answer

Dear Ms Grant,

Thank you for your email about your training event on 12 and 13 December.

I am pleased to tell you that our Garden Room is available on both days. It is our biggest meeting room and it can hold up to 30 people, so it is perfect for your group. The room has a projector, a large screen, free Wi-Fi and a flip chart, and we can also provide a microphone if you need one.

The price of the room is €300 per day. Lunch is a three-course menu and costs €22 per person, so the total for the two days is €1,480.

We also have ten double rooms available for the night of 12 December. The normal price is €80 per night, but for groups of more than eight rooms we offer a 15% discount, so each room will cost €68, including breakfast.

I would be grateful if you could confirm your booking before 15 November. If you have any other questions, please do not hesitate to contact me.

Kind regards,
Marco Silva
Events Manager, Green Park Hotel

The four tasks · circuit

Work in pairs. Student A does the four tasks and records them on a phone. Student B times and uses the partner checklist. Then change.

Tell me about yourself: your name, where you live, your job or studies, your free time, what you did last weekend and your plans for next year.

4 · Extended Conversation (about 1½ minutes)

Look at this information comparing two jobs. Which one would you prefer? Give reasons for your answer. Then talk about your current or last job. What do you like or not like about it?

Office assistant · Costa Logistics

Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:00. €1,350 a month. 10 minutes from your home. Small, friendly team.

Receptionist · Seaview Hotel

Shifts, some weekends. €1,550 a month. 40 minutes by bus. Free meals and English courses.

Model answer

I'd prefer the job at Costa Logistics. The salary is lower than at the hotel, but the timetable is much better, because I don't have to work at weekends. It's also closer to my home, so I'll spend less time and money on transport. And I think a small, friendly team is more important than free meals. On the other hand, the English courses at the hotel are a great idea, because I need English for my future. At the moment I work in a supermarket. I like my colleagues, but I don't like the timetable, because I often finish at ten at night.
