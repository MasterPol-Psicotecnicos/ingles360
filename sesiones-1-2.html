Prepositions of time

at + time: at nine, at night
on + day / date: on Monday, on 5th May
in + month / year / part of the day: in June, in 2025, in the morning

Frequency

always 100% · usually 90% · often 70% · sometimes 50% · rarely 10% · never 0%
Before the main verb: I usually walk.
After to be: I'm never late.

Text Comprehension

Read the text and answer the questions.

From: Marta López, Course Coordinator
To: New students
Subject: Welcome to the English course

Dear students,

Welcome to the English course at CAPMAN! My name is Marta and I am the course coordinator.

Classes are on Mondays and Wednesdays from six to seven in Room 4. Your teacher is María, and she is from Elche. She usually sends homework by email on Fridays.

Please bring a notebook and a pen to every class. You don't need a book. The first class is on Monday, the 3rd of November.

If you can't come to a class, please send me an email before three o'clock.

Best regards,
Marta

Speaking

Warm Up

Answer the questions for 1 minute without stopping. Record yourself on your phone.

  • What's your name? Where are you from?
  • Where do you live? Who do you live with?
  • What do you do? Where do you work or study?
  • When is your birthday?
  • What do you usually do on Saturdays? How often do you do sport?

Writing

E-mail reply

Read Marta's e-mail again and write a reply. Introduce yourself (name, age, where you live, job or studies), say what you do in your free time and how often, and ask her one question about the course. (Min. 80 words)

Checklist
Words: 0 / 80
Model answer

Dear Marta,

Thank you for your email. My name is Javier Molina and I'm 24 years old. I'm from Crevillent, but I live in Elche with my girlfriend.

I work in a supermarket from Monday to Friday, and I usually finish at five, so the class time is perfect for me. In my free time I go to the gym three times a week, and I sometimes play football with my friends on Sundays.

I have one question: do we have an exam at the end of the course?

See you on Monday,
Javier

Speaking

Warm Up · describing

Talk for 1 minute about each question. Record yourself on your phone.

  • Describe a person in your family: What does he/she look like? What is he/she like?
  • Describe your typical day: What time do you get up? What do you do in the morning, afternoon and evening?
  • What do you do at the weekend that is different?

Writing

E-mail to a friend

Your friend Sam is coming to stay with you for a week. Write an e-mail to Sam: describe your typical day (times and activities), describe one person in your family he will meet (appearance and personality), and tell him one thing about your home. (Min. 80 words)

Checklist
Words: 0 / 80
Model answer

Hi Sam,

I'm really happy you're coming next week! Here's some information about my routine. I get up at seven, have breakfast and go to work by bus. I get home at about six, and I usually go for a run before dinner.

You're going to meet my sister, Laura. She's 22, she's quite short and she's got long curly hair and brown eyes. She's very funny and talkative, so you'll like her!

Our flat is small, but there's a sofa bed in the living room for you.

See you soon,
Ana
