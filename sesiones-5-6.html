Speaking

Extended Conversation

Look at this information comparing two hotels for a work trip. Which one would you prefer? Give reasons for your answer. Then talk about the last time you stayed in a hotel. Where was it? What was it like? Did you enjoy it?

The Central Plaza

In the city centre, 5 minutes from the train station. Small but modern rooms. Free Wi-Fi. Breakfast: €12. Restaurant and bar open until midnight. €95 per night.

The Green Park Hotel

20 minutes by bus from the centre. Big, quiet rooms with a view of the park. Free breakfast and parking. Gym and swimming pool. €80 per night.

How to answer
  1. Say which one you prefer.
  2. Give two or three reasons with comparatives.
  3. Say one good thing about the other option.
  4. Answer the personal questions (past simple).
Useful language

I'd prefer… because…
X is cheaper / bigger / quieter than Y.
X isn't as … as Y.
The best thing about X is…
On the other hand, …
For a work trip, I think … is better because…

Model answer

I'd prefer the Green Park Hotel. It's cheaper than the Central Plaza, and breakfast is free, so it's much better value. The rooms are bigger and quieter, and I think that's important on a work trip because you need to sleep well. The Central Plaza is more convenient, because it's closer to the station, but the rooms are smaller and breakfast is more expensive. The last time I stayed in a hotel was in August. I went to Valencia with my family. The hotel was near the beach and the room was very comfortable. We had breakfast on the terrace every morning and I really enjoyed it.

Writing

E-mail reply · complaint

Read the following e-mail and write a reply as if you are Sam Ortiz, the manager of the Seaview Hotel. Make sure you provide all the information that Emma Clarke requests. (Min. 120 words — in the exam: 150)

To: Sam Ortiz <manager@seaviewhotel.com>
From: Emma Clarke <emma.clarke@hmail.com>
Subject: My stay last weekend

Dear Mr Ortiz,

I stayed at your hotel last weekend (Room 214). Unfortunately, the room was much smaller than in the photos on your website, and it was very noisy because it was next to the lift. Also, the breakfast was cold on both days.

Could you explain why the room was different from the photos? I would also like to know if you can give me a partial refund. Finally, I'm planning to come back in December with my husband: do you have any quieter rooms?

I look forward to hearing from you.

Emma Clarke

Before you write: what do you have to answer?
Plan: 5 short paragraphs
  1. Thank her and apologise.
  2. Explain what happened (past simple).
  3. Answer about the refund.
  4. Recommend a better room (comparatives).
  5. Close politely.
Useful language

Thank you for your email about your stay…
I'm very sorry that…
I checked your booking and…
The photos show our superior rooms, which are bigger than…
As an apology, we are happy to…
Our rooms on the fifth floor are quieter and bigger…
We look forward to welcoming you again.

Your answer

Words: 0 / 120
Model answer

Dear Ms Clarke,

Thank you for your email, and I am very sorry that you did not enjoy your stay last weekend.

I checked your booking and you are right. The photos on our website show our superior rooms, which are bigger than the standard room you stayed in. We are changing the photos this week so that our guests can see all the rooms. I also spoke to our kitchen staff about the breakfast, and it will not happen again.

As an apology, we are happy to give you a 30% refund. You will receive it in the next five days.

For your visit in December, I recommend our rooms on the fifth floor. They are quieter and bigger than Room 214, and they have a sea view. I would be happy to book one for you at a special price.

Kind regards,
Sam Ortiz
Manager, Seaview Hotel

Grammar

Talking about the future
FormUse
will
will + verb · won't + verb		Decision now, offer, promise
It's cold. I'll close the window.
Prediction (opinion)
I think it'll be a good day.
going to
am / is / are going to + verb		Plan or intention (decided before)
We're going to open a new office.
Prediction with evidence
Look at those clouds! It's going to rain.
present continuous
am / is / are + verb-ing		Fixed arrangement (date, people)
I'm meeting the client on Friday.
present simple
verb (+ -s)		Timetables
The training starts at nine.

Quick test: did you decide now? → will. Did you decide before? → going to. Is it in your diary with a time? → present continuous. Is it a timetable? → present simple.

Speaking

Reading Aloud

Read the text aloud. You have 30 seconds to prepare.

Good afternoon, everyone, and thank you for coming. As you know, next month our company is going to move to a new building on Mayor Street. The new offices are bigger and brighter than the old ones, and there will be a free car park for all employees. The move will take place on the weekend of the 14th and 15th of November. On Friday the 13th, you must put all your documents in the boxes we are going to give you. You don't have to move your computers: our IT team will do that. If you have any questions, please speak to your manager or send an email to Human Resources. We hope you will enjoy your new workplace.

How to read aloud well

Read it silently first
You have time to prepare. Read the whole text once before you speak.
Pause at . and ,
Mark short pauses with / . Don't read word by word.
Numbers and dates
the 14th → “the fourteenth” · Friday the 13th → “Friday the thirteenth”.
Natural speed
Not too fast. If you make a mistake, don't stop: correct it and continue.
Text with pauses marked

Good afternoon, / everyone, / and thank you for coming. // As you know, / next month our company is going to move to a new building on Mayor Street. // The new offices are bigger and brighter than the old ones, / and there will be a free car park for all employees. // The move will take place on the weekend of the 14th and 15th of November. // On Friday the 13th, / you must put all your documents in the boxes we are going to give you. // You don't have to move your computers: / our IT team will do that. // If you have any questions, / please speak to your manager or send an email to Human Resources. // We hope you will enjoy your new workplace.

Writing

E-mail reply · answering questions

Read the following e-mail and write a reply as if you are Alex Romero, a new employee at Brightway. Make sure you provide all the information that Laura asks for. (Min. 120 words — in the exam: 150)

To: Alex Romero <a.romero@brightway.com>
From: Laura Pérez <l.perez@brightway.com>
Subject: Your first day at Brightway

Dear Alex,

Welcome to Brightway! Your first day will be next Monday. The induction starts at 9:00 in Room 3, and lunch is included.

Before Monday, could you tell me what time you are going to arrive and how you are going to travel to the office? I also need to know if you will need a parking space and if you have any food allergies for lunch.

If you have any questions about your first day, please ask me.

Best regards,
Laura

Before you write: what do you have to answer?
Plan: 5 short paragraphs
  1. Thank her for the welcome.
  2. Arrival time and transport (going to).
  3. Parking and lunch (will / won't).
  4. Two polite questions (Could you…? Do I have to…?).
  5. Close politely.
Useful language

Thank you for your email and the warm welcome.
I'm going to arrive at about…
I'm going to come by car / train…
I will / won't need a parking space.
I don't have any food allergies, but…
Could you tell me if I have to bring…?
Is it possible to…?
I'm really looking forward to starting.

Your answer

Words: 0 / 120
Model answer

Dear Laura,

Thank you for your email and for the warm welcome. I'm really looking forward to starting at Brightway.

On Monday I'm going to arrive at about 8:45, so I'll have time to find Room 3. I'm going to come by car, because there isn't a direct bus from my town, so I will need a parking space, please.

I don't have any food allergies, but I don't eat meat. Is it possible to have a vegetarian option for lunch?

I also have two questions. Do I have to bring any documents on the first day, for example my ID card? And could you tell me what time the induction finishes? I have to pick up my son from school at five.

Thank you again. See you on Monday.

Best regards,
Alex Romero
